The house

Beauty is personal. Your experience should be too.

Luxe & Lush Aesthetics offers a range of beauty and aesthetic treatments in a welcoming, personal setting in Darlington. The menu brings together facial treatments, skin and aesthetic services, vitamin treatments, semi-permanent makeup and paired offers — all presented with the clinic’s listed prices, so you can consider what suits you before you get in touch.

Meet the owner

The person behind Luxe & Lush.

Luxe & Lush Aesthetics was established in 2025 and is owned and run by Jody Barker. The clinic is based at 19 Post House Wynd in Darlington, above Luminous Hair.

Clients describe Jody as friendly, knowledgeable and reassuring — someone who makes an appointment feel personal from the moment you arrive. If you have a question before booking, she is happy to hear from you directly.
